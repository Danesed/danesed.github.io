Research

Scalable generative models for high-dimensional, low-sample-size domains.

Danilo DanesePolitecnico di Bari · SisInfLab
Previously: Visiting researcher, University College London; Queen Square Analytics

My research combines flow- and diffusion-based synthesis for 3D data augmentation in structured multi-scale representations with multimodal semantic representations from large vision–language models, with a focus on XAI-driven model interpretability, systematic evaluation frameworks, and reproducible experimental design.

A central thread of my work is the design and validation of 3D generative models for full-resolution brain MRI, integrating multi-site preprocessing workflows and anatomy-aware evaluation as core components of the research pipeline.
